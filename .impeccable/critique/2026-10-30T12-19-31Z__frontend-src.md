## Design specificity
Category-interchangeable: six pages share one template (FilterBar, StatStrip, TrendChart, BreakdownGrid card grid). IA is organised by workbook sheet (PAL-1, MTY), not by business question. Detector: overused-font (Inter/Space Grotesk, index.css:1). In dark mode: 9 undersized-ui-text (10px labels), 5-6 low-contrast items (sidebar subtitles at opacity-60, footer).

## Priority issues
- P0 No answer layer: no "how did the month go and why". Fix: Month in Review page with headline sentence, profit bridge (prev to current NP), 4 vital signs with thresholds, exceptions list; nav renamed by question; ledgers become drill-down.
- P0 Colour semantics lie: StatStrip.tsx:82 colours the value red when it fell, so positive profit looks like a loss; null KPI becomes a red dash (StatStrip.tsx:59-60). Fix: neutral values, signed delta chips, a neutral "not uploaded" state.
- P1 No comparison below tiles: breakdown rows show a single value plus a decorative bar; rate/qty from the API are discarded. Fix: Δ MoM, Δ YoY, % of sales, ₹/kg, sorted by movers.
- P1 Numeral formatting: paise on crore figures, truncated hero values, counts shown as "97.00" (lib/format.ts:11). Fix: compact ₹ Cr/L, exact value on demand, no truncation.
- P2 Conflicting definitions: Gross Profit 24.99L (PAL-1) vs 44.30L (MTY), rupee-level NP mismatches, KPIs with no thresholds.
